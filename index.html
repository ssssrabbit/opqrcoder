How It Works

Magic hidden in plain sight

Standard QR codes contain unused "padding" bytes after the encoded data. OPQR writes your image into those bytes — invisible to scanners, visible to eyes.

1

Type your content

Enter any URL, text, Wi-Fi password, or contact info to encode.

2

Choose an image

Pick any photo from your library. Simpler images with clear contrast work best.

3

Generate

OPQR maps each image pixel to the QR padding bits and renders the result.

Generated codes become more readable using "Dither" button.

4

Save & share

Export a high-resolution PNG to your camera roll or share directly from the app.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Overview

OPQRcoder ("the App") is developed and operated as an individual project. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. The short version: the App collects no personal data and sends nothing to external servers.

Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • The text you enter to encode into a QR code is processed entirely on your device and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Images you select from your photo library are accessed only to render the OPQR code and are never saved outside your device or uploaded anywhere.
  • The App does not use analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or advertising frameworks.
  • The App does not use cookies or any equivalent tracking technologies.

Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library solely to let you select an image to embed in your QR code, and to save the generated image to your camera roll when you choose to export. This permission is optional; you can use the App without granting photo access.

In-App Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, all transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store any payment information. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or data collection. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because no personal data is collected at all, the App is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer at opqrcode@gmail.com.
